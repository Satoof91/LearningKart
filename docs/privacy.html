LearningKart – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

LearningKart is a kart game that helps young children learn Arabic and English letters, numbers and first words. It is made for children and their parents, and it is built to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. LearningKart does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and the game makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on the device

The child's progress (stars per level), the settings and the kart look are saved only on the device, in the app's own storage. They are deleted when the app is uninstalled, or with "Reset progress" in the parents' settings. On Android, the operating system may include this small save in the family's own device backup if backup is turned on. We have no access to it.

Voice

The spoken lines are recordings that ship inside the app. If a line is missing, the device's built-in text-to-speech engine may speak it. This happens on the device and nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Letters and numbers are free. Parents can unlock the full game with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see payment details or personal information. The purchase screen and the "Restore purchase" button sit behind a parents-only check.

Children

LearningKart is designed for children and complies with Google Play's Families policy and Apple's Kids category rules: no ads, no third-party analytics, no links out of the app, and a parent check before settings and purchases.
