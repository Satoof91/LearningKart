## Full description: English [Play 4000 / Apple 4000]

LearningKart turns letters into a race! Children steer a little kart down a sunny road and catch the right letter bubble. Every letter brings a new animal friend to the zoo.

**What children learn**
- 28 Arabic letters (أ to ي) and the 26 letters of the English alphabet, each with its sound and a word: «بَ، بَ، بَطَّة», "B is for bear".
- Numbers 0 to 10: count the apples on the table, one by one.
- First words: hear a word, collect its letters in order, and watch the word fill in, from «دب» and «كوب» to CAT and SUN.
- Themed word packs: animals, home, market, sea and nature.

**Made for little hands**
- Simple steering: tap or hold the left or right side of the screen.
- A friendly voice speaks every letter, number and word, and asks for the next one.
- There's no game over: a missed letter just comes back.
- Honest stars show how much help a child needed.
- Customise the kart, the driver's hat and an animal co-pilot with stars earned by learning.

**For parents**
- No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Nothing leaves the device.
- Works offline.
- Arabic or English menus, so either family language works.
- Parent settings and purchases sit behind a parents-only check.
- Letters and numbers in both languages, plus 5 words, are free. One purchase unlocks every word pack, now and in future. There are no subscriptions.
